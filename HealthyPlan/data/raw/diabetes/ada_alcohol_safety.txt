Alcohol and Diabetes
### Alcohol and Diabetes Guidelines
Drinking alcohol can cause your blood sugar to drop. This is especially true if you take insulin or certain diabetes pills that cause the body to produce more insulin. Delayed hypoglycemia is a major risk, occurring up to 24 hours after drinking. Never drink on an empty stomach. Consume food containing carbohydrates when drinking. Limit alcohol to moderate amounts: up to 1 drink per day for women, and up to 2 drinks per day for men.
