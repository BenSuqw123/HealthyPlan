### Gout and Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) Kidneys filter uric acid from the blood. In patients with CKD, the kidneys cannot filter uric acid effectively, causing hyperuricemia. This build-up of uric acid can form crystals in the joints, triggering gout attacks. Gout can also cause kidney damage or kidney stones. Managing uric acid levels below 6.0 mg/dL is critical in CKD patients to prevent joint damage and preserve renal function.
